🌀 Ceiling Fan

Mounting drop is the distance from the ceiling down to the blade's rotating plane — around 300mm for a standard fan, but up to ~1800mm on a raked/vaulted ceiling or with an extension downrod.

📏 Clearance Check

Lines and labels in the 3D view measure to the fan's centre. The pass/fail check is against clearance from the blade tip (centre distance minus the blade radius) — shown as the second line below.
