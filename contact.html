Who you're contacting

LightTools is built and maintained by one person — a lighting designer with 18 years of industry experience, working independently outside of any employer. There's no support team or ticketing system, just a real inbox. See the About page for the full story behind the site.

What to get in touch about

  • Calculator bugs or wrong results — include the calculator, the inputs you used, and what you expected
  • Regional standards corrections — if a figure doesn't match the current AS/NZS, NEC, IS or SANS standard for your region
  • Guide corrections — factual errors, outdated standards references, or broken links in any of the guides
  • New calculator or guide suggestions — what you do regularly that isn't covered yet
  • General questions about how a calculation works or which method was used

What this isn't

LightTools doesn't take on paid design or specification work directly. If you need a full lighting design done for a project in Victoria, mention that in your email and it can be pointed toward Solstice Lighting — a separate company, unrelated to this site.
