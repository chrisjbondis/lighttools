9. International Users

LightTools is used worldwide and we've built in regional support where possible. The Garden Lighting Calculator includes an 🇦🇺 Australia / 🇺🇸 United States / 🇮🇳 India region toggle that switches cable sizes, wiring standards references and Amazon product links automatically. Additional region support is planned for future updates.

All core calculations, cable sizing, voltage drop thresholds and regulatory guidance default to Australian standards and practices, including AS/NZS 3000 (Wiring Rules) and AS/NZS 1680. If you are accessing these tools from outside Australia, please be aware that electrical regulations vary significantly between countries and results may not be compliant for your jurisdiction.

In particular, users in the following regions should take note:

  • United States & Canada: Electrical installations are governed by the National Electrical Code (NEC) in the USA and the Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) in Canada. Cable sizing uses AWG (American Wire Gauge) rather than mm², voltage systems differ (120V/240V split-phase), and allowable voltage drop limits differ from Australian standards. The Garden Lighting Calculator includes a US region mode. Always verify products meet NEC/CEC requirements and carry UL, ETL or CSA listing.
  • India: Electrical installations are governed by IS 732 (wiring rules) and the Indian Electricity Rules. Cable sizing uses mm² (same as Australia) and the supply voltage is 230V/50Hz. The Garden Lighting Calculator includes an India region mode with IS 732 standards references and Amazon.in product links. Always verify compliance with local BIS standards and engage a licensed electrician.
  • United Kingdom & Europe: Electrical installations are governed by BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) in the UK and various national implementations of IEC standards across Europe. Cable sizing, circuit protection requirements and voltage drop limits differ from Australian practice. UK and EU users can use the mm² cable sizes as-is but should verify voltage drop limits against BS 7671 Part 5.
  • South Africa: Electrical installations are governed by SANS 10142 (Wiring of Premises). The supply voltage is 230V/50Hz and cable sizing uses mm² — fully compatible with these tools. The Garden Lighting Calculator includes a South Africa region mode with SANS 10142 standards references and Takealot product links. Always verify compliance with local regulations and use a registered electrician (registered with the Department of Labour).
  • Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Zimbabwe & Other African Countries: Most African countries follow IEC 60364 or BS 7671 (inherited from UK practice), use 230V/50Hz and size cables in mm². Results from these tools are broadly applicable — verify local voltage drop limits, circuit protection requirements and licensing rules with a qualified local electrician.
  • Singapore & Malaysia: Both follow SS 638 (Singapore) and MS IEC 60364 (Malaysia) which are closely aligned with IEC standards. Cable sizing in mm² is compatible with these tools. Verify local derating factors and installation requirements.
  • New Zealand: New Zealand shares AS/NZS 3000 with Australia. Results from these tools are broadly applicable, however local licensing requirements and some specific clauses differ. Always verify with a licensed New Zealand electrician before proceeding.

International users are solely responsible for verifying that any calculations, cable sizes, driver ratings or wiring layouts produced by these tools comply with their local electrical codes, standards and regulations before use.
